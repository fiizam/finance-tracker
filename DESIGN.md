# Design Direction

## Identity & Personality
- **Identity**: Personal money journal / personal finance companion
- **Personality**: Clean, modern, fresh, human-designed, premium but understated

## Typography
- Excellent typography, strong hierarchy
- Generous whitespace

## Palette
- Neutral surfaces
- **One** carefully selected accent color (used deliberately for focus)

## Composition & Layout
- Thin borders, restrained corner radius, minimal shadows
- Avoid huge hero sections, glowing cards, floating blobs, glassmorphism
- Information hierarchy is paramount

## Dials
- **ENERGY**: 2 (Subtle but deliberate)
- **RHYTHM**: 3 (Varied layouts according to content)
- **MOTION**: 2 (Refined and subtle, 60fps, CSS transitions over heavy animations, subtle parallax)

## Special Instructions
- Final interface must NOT look AI-generated (no generic neon, no AI placeholders, no fake stats).
- Use typography, spacing, alignment, and hierarchy as the primary visual system instead of putting everything in cards.
